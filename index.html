Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does a standard bathroom tile installation take?

A typical bathroom tile project usually takes between 3 to 5 days depending on the scope of work, including surface preparation, waterproofing, tile laying, grouting, and curing time.

2. What is the difference between ceramic and porcelain tile?

Porcelain tile is denser, less porous, and more water-resistant than standard ceramic tile, making it an excellent choice for shower stalls and high-traffic floor tile installation.

3. Can you repair or replace broken bathroom tiles without replacing the whole floor?

Yes! Our bathroom tile replacement service can isolate damaged or cracked tiles and swap them out cleanly without disturbing the surrounding floor or wall tile structure.

4. Do you provide waterproofing services prior to tile setting?

Absolutely. Proper waterproofing membrane application is a fundamental step in all our shower tile installation and wet area projects to ensure long-term structural protection.
